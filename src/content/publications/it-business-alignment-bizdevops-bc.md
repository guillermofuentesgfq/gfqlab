Organizations adopting DevOps usually find that alignment with the business is
the part that degrades first. This paper proposes BizDevOps-BC, a business
capability for teams moving from DevOps to BizDevOps without losing delivery
agility.

**The capability has four pillars**: people, processes, information, and the
resources to develop software under BizDevOps. Each pillar carries a minimum
requirement rather than a best practice, so the artifact answers a question a
team can actually act on — *what is missing here?* — instead of a question that
has no fixed answer.

**Validation.** A proof of concept in a DevOps organization already running
SAFe, plus an expert survey of industry practitioners and academics. The result
worth stating plainly: BizDevOps-BC is a complement, not a replacement. An
organization that adopted DevOps and SAFe does not need to restructure to adopt
BizDevOps; it needs to fill specific gaps. That is a much lower-risk claim than
the one the name of the approach sometimes implies.
