An early paper in this line of work, and the one that named the tension the rest
of it tries to resolve: BizDevOps asks for more business involvement in software
delivery, and asks for agility at the same time.

Those two goals pull against each other in predictable places — governance
gates that lengthen cycles, requirements that harden once business stakeholders
are in the room, and alignment efforts that quietly become waterfall. The
contribution here is a structured account of where that happens, which is the
precondition for the business capability model that came later in
[Improving IT/Business Alignment in DevOps](/publications/it-business-alignment-bizdevops-bc).
