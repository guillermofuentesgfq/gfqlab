A systematic mapping study over 86 primary and 11 secondary studies, asking
whether enterprise architecture and IT governance proposals actually exist that
integrate both into a BizDevOps context.

**The finding is a gap, not a solution.** No proposal in the corpus addresses
BizDevOps, enterprise architecture and IT governance together; the literature has
combinations of two, not three. The study is also blunt about the shape of what
exists: a lack of empirical validation, and a predominance of
recommendation-oriented papers that propose nothing that could be applied.

**On the notation.** Where models do appear, ArchiMate is the one that fits —
because BizDevOps maps onto its three layers, which are also the three cycles of
the approach (business, application, technology). BPMN appears alongside it for
process concerns, but it cannot carry the motivation elements that make
alignment visible to stakeholders.
