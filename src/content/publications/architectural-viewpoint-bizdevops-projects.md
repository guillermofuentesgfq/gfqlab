BizDevOps-VP: an ArchiMate architectural viewpoint for managing software projects
under BizDevOps, designed with Design Science Research.

**The problem it addresses.** A viewpoint is what lets a mixed team — business
stakeholders, product, engineering — discuss the same concerns without
everyone modelling a different thing. Without one, alignment conversations turn
into vocabulary disagreements.

**The specification.** Layers: business, application and technology, excluding
the physical layer. Aspects: passive structure, active structure, behavior and
motivation. The exclusion is deliberate; so is including motivation, because the
*why* of a change is the part business stakeholders need to see and the part
engineering diagrams normally leave out.

**On the audience.** The artifact is aimed at people responsible for BizDevOps
initiatives who may have no background in enterprise architecture. That
constraint shaped the whole design more than the ArchiMate specification did.
